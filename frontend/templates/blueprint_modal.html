Six-Stage Cognitive Processing Pipeline
1
Lexical & Morphological Ingestion

Powered by spaCy (en_core_web_sm). Performs sentence segmentation, subword-aware tokenization, part-of-speech (POS) tagging, lemmatization, and named-entity normalization.

2
Multi-Head Intent Classification

Parses student query intent (ASK_DEFINITION, ASK_EXPLANATION, ASK_COMPARISON, ASK_EXAMPLE, GENERAL_QUERY) using regular expression heuristics and semantic centroid distance.

3
Dense & Sparse RAG Knowledge Retrieval

Hybrid search combining TF-IDF lexical frequency and dense embedding cosine similarity. Pulls grounded academic excerpts from the syllabus corpus and student lecture notes.

4
Socratic Scaffolding & Bloom's Taxonomy

Answers are calibrated to Bloom's cognitive taxonomy levels (Remembering → Understanding → Applying → Analyzing). Encourages conceptual self-discovery rather than raw spoon-feeding.

5
Multimodal Viva & Teach-Back Rubric

Evaluates student oral defense and Feynman teach-back explanations across 5 weighted dimensions: Conceptual Accuracy, Depth, Clarity, Analogy Usage, and Technical Keyword Density.

6
SuperMemo-2 Spaced Repetition

Memory consolidation algorithm dynamically calculates retention intervals ($I$) and easiness factors ($EF$), preventing the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve.

Core Mathematical Formulations
1. Cosine Similarity in Dense Vector Space
cos(θ) = (u • v) / (||u|| × ||v||) = ∑(u_i × v_i) / ( √∑(u_i²) × √∑(v_i²) )
Calculates semantic proximity between student queries and indexed academic knowledge vectors in normalized hyperspace.
2. Term Frequency – Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF)
TF-IDF(t, d, D) = TF(t, d) × [ log( (1 + |D|) / (1 + |{d ∈ D : t ∈ d}|) ) + 1 ]
Quantifies lexical importance of domain keywords while down-weighting ubiquitous grammatical terms.
3. SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) Easiness Factor Update
EF' = EF + [ 0.1 - (5 - q) × (0.08 + (5 - q) × 0.02) ], where EF' ≥ 1.3
Where q ∈ [0, 5] represents student recall grade. Controls flashcard review intervals: $I(1) = 1$, $I(2) = 6$, $I(n) = I(n-1) \times EF$ (capped at 365 days max).
4. Weighted Multi-Criterion Rubric Scoring
Score = ∑(w_i × s_i), where ∑ w_i = 1.0
Instructor-configurable weights: Accuracy (35%), Completeness (25%), Terminology (20%), Clarity (10%), Defense Confidence (10%).
